Guildhollow: Idle RPG Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Guildhollow: Idle RPG ("Guildhollow," "the game," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile game developed and published by Hollowfern.

Information the game may process

Guildhollow may process limited information needed to operate game features, save progress, provide online services, prevent abuse, process purchases, and display advertising. Depending on the features and third-party services enabled in the version you use, this may include:

  • Game progress, settings, inventory, achievements, quest progress, and other gameplay data.
  • Device and technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language, identifiers, IP address, crash information, diagnostics, and performance data.
  • Advertising-related information and identifiers when advertising services are enabled.
  • Purchase and transaction information associated with in-app purchases. Payment card details are processed by the applicable app store or payment provider and are not directly collected by Guildhollow.
  • Online-service identifiers or account-related identifiers generated or supplied by platform and game-service providers when those features are used.

Guildhollow does not intentionally require users to provide sensitive personal information to play the game.

How information is used

  • Operate and maintain Guildhollow.
  • Save and restore game progress and provide online game functionality.
  • Deliver and measure advertising, including rewarded advertising.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases.
  • Diagnose crashes, bugs, security issues, fraud, and technical problems.
  • Improve game performance, balancing, reliability, and features.
  • Comply with applicable law and platform requirements.

Third-party services

Guildhollow may use third-party platform, game-service, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, and payment services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies in order to provide their services. Depending on the build and platform, these services may include Google Play services and Unity services.

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the game, provide advertising or purchases, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Advertising

Guildhollow may contain advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, interaction data, and related technical information to deliver, measure, secure, and, where permitted, personalize advertisements. Consent or privacy choices will be provided where required by applicable law or platform policy.

Children's privacy

Guildhollow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If we learn that personal information from a child was collected in a manner requiring deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Parents or guardians may contact us using the privacy contact method provided for the game or its store listing.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures, together with the security features provided by our platform and service providers, to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay and service data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the game, provide requested features, maintain security and integrity, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may also depend on the practices of third-party service providers.

Users may request deletion of personal data controlled by Hollowfern by contacting the privacy contact associated with Guildhollow's Google Play listing. Data controlled independently by an app store or third-party provider may need to be deleted through that provider.

If Guildhollow later introduces user-created accounts, we will provide any account-deletion mechanism required by applicable platform rules and law.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for such processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Guildhollow changes. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material revisions are made. Continued use of the game after an update is subject to the revised policy as permitted by law.
